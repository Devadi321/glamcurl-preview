Benefits

What your own app does for your shop

When a shop owner visits this page, this is the pitch: real, practical benefits — not tech jargon.

Free push notifications

Ping your customers' lock screens about offers, slow-day deals or restocks — free, instant, and way more effective than SMS or email.

Loyalty & rewards built in

Digital stamp cards and points turn one-time buyers into regulars who come back on their own — automatically.

Ordering & booking, 24/7

Customers order, book or reserve any time — even when your shutters are down. You wake up to confirmed sales.

Zero commission
